**Microscale Integrated Sensing, Actuation, and Smart Systems (MISAS) Lab** led by Prof. Xiangyu ZHAO at the [City University of Hong Kong (Dongguan)](https://www.cityu-dg.edu.cn/en/home.html) , focuses on the co-design, microfabrication, and system-level validation of integrated MEMS and microsystems for intelligent, autonomous, sensing systems.
 
*Research directions:* System integration, microscale gas chromatography (μGC), energy harvesting, low-power power management electronics, thin film functional materials for MEMS, packaging and thermal co-design, AI-assisted microsystem optimization, wireless self-powered sensing nodes.

*Call for collaboration:* Actively hiring both PhD and Masters Students. We welcome students who are interested in cutting edge research in micro/nano devices and microsystems, want to pursue a scientific research career, and are proficient in English. Please send an email with the subject of  Application for position - Your Name - Your Major/Area containing a brief introduction, CV, and other materials you feel are relevant. 

*Call for collaboration:* Cross- and multidiscipline research on MEMS/micro-nano devices, system integration, functional materials, energy harvesting, IoT and distributed sensing, lab-on-chip chemical analysis, low-power embedded electronics, packaging reliability, smart system co-design (AI for microsystems) and microsystem design for autonomous sensing.
